Bitmap fonts for platforms without the Windows 3.1 system fonts: Wine's free
re-creations of System, Fixedsys, MS Sans Serif, Small Fonts and Courier, converted
to Windows FNT files by tools/winefonts.py. They come from Wine 5.0
(https://gitlab.winehq.org/wine/wine, fonts/*.ttf, built from fonts/*.sfd) and are
distributed under the GNU Lesser General Public License 2.1 (COPYING.LIB).
